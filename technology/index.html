The Case for Accessible Plumbing

Pipes eventually fail. What makes a failure extraordinarily expensive is where the system is located and when the building discovers the problem.

Accessible starts as a routing decision.

The new system is given a deliberate path around the building. That decision reduces dependence on concealed cavities and shapes how installation, finishing, and future access work together.

The finished system

Fewer holes in the building. More thought in the route.

Plumbing Track is an enclosed architectural system, not exposed pipe. Planned routing is intended to reduce unnecessary openings while allowing required fire-separation crossings to be properly detailed.
