Solutions / Occupied Building Repiping

Repipe the building around the people in it.

Occupied-building repiping is an operations problem as much as a construction problem. Resident communication, suite access, protection, daily sequencing, and finishing all belong in the plan.

The operational difference

Residents stay home while the building moves forward.

The work has to move through many homes while residents, property teams, and crews share the same building. Plumbing Track plans those handoffs as part of the project instead of treating them as an afterthought.

The goal is not to promise zero disruption. It is to keep each phase deliberate and reduce unnecessary opening and restoration wherever the building allows.

Before suite entry

Set expectations and access.

Plan communication, access windows, protection, staging, and the practical details residents and property teams need.

During the work

Keep each visit focused.

Use the planned route and work sequence to concentrate activity in the spaces that actually need access.

At handoff

Return a finished room.

Complete the visible enclosure and finishes so the work becomes part of the building rather than an ongoing construction condition.
