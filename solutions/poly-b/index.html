Solutions / Poly-B Replacement

A better route for a known problem.

Poly-B replacement is a building decision, not only a pipe decision. Plumbing Track plans the replacement system around occupied homes, finished conditions, and future access.

The problem

Replace the material without reopening the whole building.

When a building needs Poly-B replacement, the project has to account for the existing routing, residents, access, restoration, and the system that will remain afterward.

An assessment is the place to establish what is known about the pipe and what the building actually needs. Do not force a diagnosis when the material is uncertain.

Understand

Start with the existing building.

Map the pipe material, layout, occupancy, constraints, and project priorities.

Replace

Plan the new route.

Use an enclosed accessible route rather than automatically burying the replacement system again.

Finish

Leave the building ready for what comes next.

Coordinate the finished condition and future service access as part of the project.
