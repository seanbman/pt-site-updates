Respond to Kitec with a system plan.

Kitec replacement involves the pipe, fittings, building layout, residents, and the restoration that follows. Plumbing Track connects those decisions through one planned route.

The problem

Pipe failure is only part of the project.

Kitec pipe and fittings can be difficult to reach inside a multifamily building. The replacement approach needs to account for where the system is located, how the work is sequenced, and how the finished building will be maintained.

Use an assessment to confirm the building conditions and scope. Exact project facts, risk language, and quantitative outcomes remain tied to verified source evidence.
