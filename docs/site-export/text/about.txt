WHO we are

The Modern Standard for Multifamily Repiping

After completing two large multifamily condo repipe projects using conventional methods, it was clear the industry needed a faster, cleaner, less disruptive approach. The work got done—but the process felt outdated.

That frustration sparked an idea.

The concept was developed from real-world experience and brought to life with 3D modeling, then taken directly to manufacturing to create the first-generation extrusion dies.

The result was Plumbing Track: a modern repiping system designed to reduce disruption, speed up installs, and deliver consistent, high-quality results in occupied buildings.

“Conventional methods are gone. We had to move on — so we built a better solution.”

— Paul Jahn, Founder

Our approach

One system. One team. One complete solution.

From assessment and design to permits, engineering, plumbing, construction, and final finishes, we manage every step to keep repiping predictable, efficient, and clean.

On schedule, on budget

Our mission

To provide multifamily condominiums with a cleaner, aesthetically refined, and more efficient way to repipe their buildings — designed as a long-term, future-ready solution.
