Solutions / Occupied Building Repiping

Repipe the building around the people in it.

Occupied-building repiping is a coordination problem as much as a construction problem. The route, resident communication, access, protection, and finished condition all belong in the plan.

The operational difference

Residents stay home while the building moves forward.

Plumbing Track is designed for multifamily buildings where residents, owners, property managers, and crews must work through the same project sequence.

The goal is not to promise zero disruption. It is to plan the work around occupied homes and reduce unnecessary opening and restoration wherever the building allows.

Prepare

Coordinate residents and access.

Plan communication, suite entry, protection, staging, and the daily work sequence.

Install

Use the building deliberately.

Route and install the replacement system through occupied spaces with a clear finished-condition plan.

Complete

Leave an enclosed route and a system designed with future service access in mind.
