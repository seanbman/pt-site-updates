## Documentation

### `documentation/README.md`
Top-level handoff guide for `website-media-0926`. Explains the purpose of the collection, how the curated media should be approached, and the relationship between the media review and the current website plan.

### `documentation/photo-curation-readme.md`
Detailed notes from the still-image review. Includes curation rationale, recommended website uses, editing guidance, and cautions around project identity, privacy, and claims.

### `documentation/PHOTO_INVENTORY.json`
Machine-readable inventory of the reviewed still-photo material. Intended for development, automation, future media management, and tracing curation decisions back to individual assets.

### `documentation/VIDEO_CURATION.md`
Video review and website-placement guide. Summarizes the strongest footage, recommended page/section usage, editing approach, evidence gaps, and limitations of the review.

Important limitation documented there: 66 of 88 source MP4s were transferable through the Drive connector and visually reviewed across their runtimes. The remaining 22 exceeded the connector's approximately 256 MiB per-file transfer limit and are identified as unreviewed rather than being assigned a quality grade. Presenter audio was not transcribed in the review environment, so spoken marketing or technical claims still require verification before publication.

### `documentation/review-material/video-review-material.zip`
Review support package for the video audit. Contains generated filmstrips/contact sheets and metadata used to inspect the transferable video library efficiently. This is primarily a review/reference resource, not website-ready media.

## Recommended Use Order

For implementation work, start with:

1. `documentation/README.md` for the handoff context.
2. `documentation/VIDEO_CURATION.md` and `documentation/photo-curation-readme.md` for placement guidance.
3. `images/priority-image-contact-sheet.jpg` for rapid still-image selection.
4. `videos/priority-originals/` for immediately usable source footage.
5. The ZIP packages and inventories when deeper review or source tracing is needed.

## Website Direction Carried Into This Handoff

The media curation was evaluated against the current `pt-site-updates` website direction. The existing homepage hero video should remain in place rather than being silently replaced. New media is best used to strengthen the site's **Problem → System → Proof** story, particularly the Technology, Accessible Plumbing, Detailed Installation, Occupied Building Repiping, and Our Work material.

Use real installation evidence conservatively. Do not infer Poly B/Kitec project identity, resident/project details, future-service claims, or presenter-spoken claims unless those facts are separately verified from source records.
