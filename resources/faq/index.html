Common questions

Clear answers before the project starts.

What is Plumbing Track?

Plumbing Track is a modern repiping system designed around occupied multifamily buildings, with an enclosed accessible route and a finished architectural result.

Can residents stay home during a repipe?

The project is designed around occupied homes, resident communication, access, protection, and a planned work sequence. The exact requirements depend on the building and approved scope.

Does accessible mean exposed pipe?

No. The pipe is enclosed within the Plumbing Track system. Accessible means the route and future service access are deliberately designed into the finished building.

Will the whole building need to be opened?

The route is planned to reduce unnecessary wall and ceiling disturbance. Required openings and fire-separation detailing still depend on the building and project scope.

How do I know what my building needs?

Start with an assessment. Share what you know about the building, project, occupancy, and pipe material, including an Unsure response when the material is not known.
