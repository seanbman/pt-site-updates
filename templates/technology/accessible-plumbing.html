Accessible plumbing

The Case for Accessible Plumbing

Pipes eventually fail. What makes a failure extraordinarily expensive is where the system is located and when the building discovers the problem.

Every pipe in a wall is a pipe you can’t inspect.

The expensive failures are the late ones.

Frame accessibility as earlier discovery and serviceability. Do not claim Plumbing Track prevents pipe failure.

Work done in the open is better work.

Use real installation photography to explain planned visible runs, routing, joints, and support spacing.

You get to choose the route.

Show how a planned route can be designed around the building rather than inheriting cavity constraints.
