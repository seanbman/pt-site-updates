Accessible plumbing

The Case for Accessible Plumbing

Pipes eventually fail. What makes a failure extraordinarily expensive is where the system is located and when the building discovers the problem.

Every pipe in a wall is a pipe you can’t inspect.

The expensive failures are the late ones.

Accessibility makes the route easier to understand and service. Plumbing Track is designed to improve access, not to prevent pipe failure.

Work done in the open is better work.

Installation photography shows how planned visible runs, routing, joints, and support spacing come together.

You get to choose the route.

A planned route can follow the building’s own lines instead of inheriting every constraint of a wall cavity.
