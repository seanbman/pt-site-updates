Why Housing Authorities Choose Plumbing Track

No One Has to Move Out

Traditional repiping forces weeks or months of relocation, hotel stays, and massive tenant disruption. With the Plumbing Track System, residents stay in their units for the entire project. Life goes on as normal: no packing, no temporary housing costs, and no lost rental income for you.

No Dust. No Mess. Minimal Disruption

Our surface-mounted track system installs along ceilings and corridors like modern crown molding. We avoid opening walls and ceilings in living spaces, which means:

  • Dramatically reduced dust and debris
  • Faster project timelines
  • Happier tenants and fewer complaints
  • Lower cleaning and restoration costs

No Ceiling Damage, and a Major Asbestos Advantage

Many older BC affordable housing buildings contain asbestos in ceilings and insulation. Traditional repiping often requires disturbing these materials, triggering expensive abatement, permits, and safety protocols.

Plumbing Track’s non-invasive approach minimizes or completely eliminates ceiling disturbance, significantly reducing asbestos-related risks, costs, and regulatory hurdles. Safer for workers, tenants, and your organization.
