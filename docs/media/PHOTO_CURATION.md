## Edit policy before publishing

Use conservative documentary edits first: normalize EXIF orientation, straighten/perspective-correct where useful, crop to make the Plumbing Track route dominant, apply light exposure/white-balance correction, and export responsive WebP/AVIF derivatives. Avoid beautifying away construction facts or changing the plumbing installation itself.

A privacy pass is mandatory. Several corridor images visibly contain suite numbers, posted work sheets, or other unit-identifying material. Several suite interiors contain residents' possessions. Crop or blur those details before public release. `20260420_101749.jpg` includes people around the pool and is not recommended for the current site.

For finished-room images, favor crops of the upper architectural band so the enclosure reads as intentional trim while retaining enough room context to prove it is a real installation. For process images, keep enough surrounding wall/room context to show that openings are localized; overly tight crops would weaken the point.

Do not use the exterior photographs as named project proof until the building/project identity and publication permission are confirmed. Do not use this set as Poly-B- or Kitec-specific proof until the source project and original pipe material are verified.

## Important evidence gap

The new photos do **not** clearly show a finished service/access panel being opened or serviced. That means they do not cleanly prove the current Detailed Installation step **“Leave future access.”** Keep the existing image for that slot unless a stronger verified access photo exists. This is preferable to forcing a visually attractive but semantically inaccurate image into the step.
