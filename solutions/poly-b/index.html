Solutions / Poly-B Replacement

Poly-B replacement for occupied buildings.

Aging polybutylene is a building-wide problem. The replacement plan has to deal with the pipe, the suites it passes through, and the amount of finished building that must be opened to reach it.

The problem

Do not let the next leak choose the project schedule.

Poly-B was widely installed in Canadian buildings from the late 1970s through the late 1990s. It can deteriorate internally while looking normal from the outside, which makes a building-wide replacement plan more useful than chasing failures one at a time.

The first step is to confirm what the building has, where it runs, and whether previous partial repairs have left a mixed system.
