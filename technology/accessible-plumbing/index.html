Make access part of the system.

A replacement pipe can be new and still be buried away. Accessible plumbing changes the route so the finished building is easier to understand, inspect, and service.

Not exposed

Enclosed, planned, and built into the finish.

The track encloses the new pipe in a clean, deliberate route. It gives the system a defined place in the finished room while keeping access part of the conversation.

The building still needs proper detailing, maintenance, and professional service. Accessible plumbing helps those tasks begin with a known route.

In the finished room
